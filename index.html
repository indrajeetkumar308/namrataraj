बस एक मिनट, अपने लिए।

Before anything else — let's breathe together.

Your breath is the fastest way to tell your nervous system, "I am safe." Follow the circle for one minute. Breathe in as it grows, gently out as it softens.

  • • In for 4  ·  hold for 4  ·  out slowly for 6
  • • Longer out-breaths help the body shift out of alarm mode
